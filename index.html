About the collection

Women, Fall/Winter 2024. Graduate collection by Oumaima Rihane.

Embracing emotional exposure and authenticity, weaving together sustainable patchwork fabrics from various sources. Bold colours and unexpected combinations make Hidden Radiance a social commentary, celebrating vulnerability as a profound source of strength and self-expression.

“A self-expression of a social commentary, exposed as a celebration of vulnerability.”

Six looks, photographed as they move.

Scroll through each look and the model moves with you, one frame at a time, the way the lookbook was shot against a hand-painted wall.

Hidden Depth

Unspoken Exposure

The power of the eyes as a symbol of strength and vulnerability. I aim to evoke the past and present struggles of my culture. The accessories serve as an expression of resilience, embodying the notion that true strength lies in embracing our vulnerabilities.

Through Unspoken Exposure, I invite you to explore the hidden depths of the soul, where vulnerability becomes a source of empowerment.

Grounded Glow

Embracing Diversity

Embracing diversity as a powerful expression of vulnerability. Unique forms, races and characters. The focus on diverse eye shapes highlights individual narratives of strength and resilience.

Standing united in the beauty of our shared humanity.

The virtual gallery

Le Riad

Step inside a riad built for the collection, at dusk. A black reflecting pool with floating candles, horseshoe arcades in polished tadelakt, carved stucco and pierced brass lanterns. Around the courtyard, four galleries with hand-brushed walls, the same as the wall every look was photographed against, each work under its own light.

The studio

Radiance Creative Studio works with fashion brands, artists and cultural institutions, crafting authentic narratives and striking visuals rooted in Moroccan identity.

  • Artistic direction

    Creative consulting for brands and artists, from first concept to the finished campaign.

  • Styling and lookbooks

    Fashion styling, lookbook creation and brand storytelling.

  • Photography and film

    Photo and video production for campaigns and product drops.

  • Campaign design

    Campaigns for fashion and lifestyle brands.

  • Costume design

    Costume design and styling for shows, stage and screen.

  • Collaborations

    Joint projects with artists and media outlets across Morocco, Africa and the Middle East.
